HOW TO PLAY
🎯
Use the Wheel Letters
Form words using only the letters in the outer ring. Each letter can only be used as many times as it appears on the wheel.
⭕
Centre Letter is Mandatory
Every word must contain the centre letter (the highlighted middle tile). No exceptions — words without it are invalid.
📏
4 Letters Minimum
Words must be 4 or more letters long. Short words don't count.
📖
Macquarie Dictionary Only
Words are checked against a standard English dictionary. Made-up words, proper nouns, and abbreviations are rejected.
🚫
No Plurals
If a word has already been played, its plural form is also blocked. No sneaky S on the end!
⏱️
40 Second Timer
You have 40 seconds per turn. The bar and ring count down. At 10 seconds the alarm fires — get moving!
🔄
Taking Turns
Players alternate turns. First round special rule: if Player 1 fails or times out, Player 2 gets one chance to save themselves.
🏆
Last Player Standing Wins
The player who makes an invalid word, uses a word already played, or runs out of time loses. The other player wins!
